About This Project

This project uses YOLOv8 deep learning models to detect and classify cracks in concrete structures such as highways and bridges. It supports both image and video inputs, providing annotated outputs and inspection reports.

Developed for National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) internship use cases, the system demonstrates AI-powered infrastructure monitoring and proactive maintenance planning.

How to Use

  1. Click on Launch Detection App above.
  2. Upload an image or short video of a concrete surface.
  3. View annotated results with detected cracks.
  4. Download inspection reports for documentation.
